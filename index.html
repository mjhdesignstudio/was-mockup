Welcome to East Hill Worship Arts School 👋🏻

We are a community of artists and musicians dedicated to worship and creativity. Our school offers a variety of classes and workshops to help you grow in your artistic skills and deepen your worship experience.

Classes are offered 3-4 times per year at the East Hill Church Family Campus in Gresham, Oregon.

After our morning session, we gather for lunch and fellowship and then spend time worshipping together giving each student the chance to put into practice the skills and techniques they just learned.

Currently the following courses are offered:
  • Vocal Lab
  • Piano Lab
  • Guitar Lab (Electric and Acoustic)
  • Bass Lab
  • Drum Lab
  • Tech Lab (Front of House, Pro Presenter, Cameras)
